The problem

Every spring, students moving out of their college homes need to get rid of couches, desks, lamps, rugs and wall decor fast. Much of it ends up on the curb or in a dumpster. At the same time, incoming students and people signing new leases are paying full retail for the same items a few blocks away. Facebook Marketplace and group chats are cluttered, aren't limited to students, and make it hard to trust who you're meeting.

The solution

Reduce, Reuse, CampusSupplies is a website built only for students at your school. You sign up with a .edu email, so every buyer and seller is a verified student. Listings are organized around the realities of college living: move-out dates, pickup distance from campus, and what fits in a dorm room or a shared apartment.

How it works

  1. Verify

    Create an account with your school email.

  2. List or browse

    Post an item with photos, price and the dates it's available, or search by category, price and distance.

  3. Message and meet

    Chat in the app and agree on a pickup time and place.

  4. Hand off

    Pay through the site or in person and mark the item as sold.

Key features

Student-only access

.edu verification keeps the marketplace local and trusted.

Move-out calendar

Sellers set a "must be gone by" date so buyers can plan around leases.

Distance filters

See how far each item is from campus or your own address.

Room bundles

Sell a whole room at once: bed frame, desk, lamp and rug together.

Who it's for

Sellers

Graduating seniors and students moving out who want quick cash and an easy way to clear out.

Buyers

Freshmen, transfer students and anyone furnishing a new place on a student budget.

How it makes money

Listing is free. Reduce, Reuse, CampusSupplies takes a small fee (about 5%) on payments made through the site and charges a few dollars to feature a listing at the top of search during busy move-in and move-out weeks. Later, it can partner with local movers and storage companies for referral fees.

Why it matters

Reduce, Reuse, CampusSupplies saves students money, makes moving less stressful and keeps usable furniture out of landfills. Because demand peaks at predictable times each year (move-out in May, move-in in August), the site can focus its marketing on a few weeks and grow one campus at a time.
